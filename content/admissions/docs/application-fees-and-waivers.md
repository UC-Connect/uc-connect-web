# UC Application Fees and Fee Waivers

One UC application is used to apply to any of the nine undergraduate campuses. First-year and transfer applicants pay a fee for each campus selected.

## Application fee

- $80 per UC campus.
- $95 per campus for international and non-immigrant applicants.
- Fees are non-refundable. Once the application is submitted, the applicant must pay for every campus selected, even if the application is later cancelled.

## Fee waivers

- UC waives application fees for up to four campuses for qualified students who would otherwise be unable to apply.
- The fee waiver program is for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and applicants eligible for AB 540 benefits.
- Waivers cannot cover more than four campuses regardless of their source. Each additional campus beyond four costs $80.
- There is no separate fee waiver form. Applicants enter family size and income in the "About you" section, and the application shows in the "Campuses & majors" section how many campuses they can apply to for free.

## What to have ready

UC lists these items to have ready: transcripts for reference (they are not submitted), annual income for last year and the current year (optional unless applying for a fee waiver or the Educational Opportunity Program), a Social Security number if the applicant has one, and citizenship or immigration status.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not promise a student will receive a fee waiver; the application determines eligibility.
- Do not say waivers can cover more than four campuses.
- Do not ask the user for their Social Security number or income details.
