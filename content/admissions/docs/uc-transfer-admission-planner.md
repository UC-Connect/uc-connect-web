# UC Transfer Admission Planner

UC TAP is an online planning tool for prospective UC transfer students. Students can enter academic history and planned coursework to track preparation.

TAG applications are submitted through UC TAP for participating campuses. UC TAP is a planning and communication tool; it is not the regular UC admission application.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not say that creating a UC TAP account is the same as applying to UC.
- Do not say that entering courses in UC TAP guarantees transferability or admission.
