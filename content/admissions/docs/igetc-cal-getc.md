# IGETC and Cal-GETC

IGETC and Cal-GETC are general education patterns for California community college students.

UC states that:
- IGETC may be available to students who enrolled at a California community college before fall 2025;
- beginning fall 2025, Cal-GETC is the general education pattern for newly enrolling California community college students;
- UC will continue accepting valid IGETC certifications.

Completion of IGETC or Cal-GETC is not automatically required for every UC transfer applicant. Suitability may depend on the campus, college, school, and major. Some high-unit or highly structured majors may prioritize major preparation.

Students should verify their applicable pattern and certification status with their California community college counselor.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not tell every transfer student that IGETC is mandatory.
- Do not assume IGETC and Cal-GETC apply to non-CCC students.
- Do not recommend a pattern for a specific major without checking campus guidance.
