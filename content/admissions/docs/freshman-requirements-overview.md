# UC First-Year (Freshman) Admission Requirements

## Who applies as a first-year student

UC considers you a first-year (freshman) applicant if you are currently in high school, or if you have graduated from high school but have not yet enrolled in a regular fall, winter, or spring session at a college or university.

College courses taken while in high school, or in the summer after high school graduation, do not change first-year applicant status.

## Minimum requirements

To meet UC's minimum first-year requirements, applicants must:
- complete at least 15 yearlong college-preparatory (A-G) courses with a grade of C or better, with at least 11 of them finished before the start of the last year of high school;
- earn a GPA of 3.0 or better in A-G courses for California residents, or 3.4 or better for nonresidents, with no grade lower than a C.

Meeting the minimum requirements makes a student eligible to be considered. It does not guarantee admission to any campus or major.

## Admission by exception

Admission by exception is a route for applicants who do not meet the A-G course requirement or the minimum GPA requirement. UC states that each campus may admit only "a few" students this way. It is intended for students with strong potential, such as home-schooled students without transcripts or students whose life circumstances affected their academic record. Applicants can explain their situation in the personal insight questions or the additional comments section.

## Letters of recommendation and transcripts

Letters of recommendation are not required or read at the application stage. A campus may request them later in a supplemental review.

Applicants self-report their courses and grades on the application. Official transcripts are not sent with the application; admitted students submit final official transcripts to their campus.

## Answer guardrails

- Never describe the 3.0 or 3.4 minimum GPA as a competitive GPA.
- Always distinguish minimum UC eligibility from campus or major selection.
- Do not say admission by exception is a likely or reliable route; UC says only a few students per campus are admitted this way.
