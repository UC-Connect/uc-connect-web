# UC Transfer Basic Requirements

For junior-level transfer eligibility, UC states that applicants generally must complete at least 60 semester units or 90 quarter units of UC-transferable college credit.

Applicants must complete UC's 7-course pattern by the end of the spring term before fall enrollment at UC:
- two transferable courses in English composition (UC-E);
- one transferable course in mathematical concepts and quantitative reasoning (UC-M);
- four transferable college courses chosen from at least two of arts and humanities (UC-H), social and behavioral sciences (UC-B), and physical and biological sciences (UC-S).

The minimum UC-transferable GPA is generally:
- 2.4 for California residents;
- 2.8 for nonresidents.

Some campuses and selective majors require substantially higher GPAs.

No more than 14 semester units or 21 quarter units of the required transferable credit may generally be taken Pass/No Pass or Credit/No Credit, subject to UC rules and stated exceptions.

Applicants generally need a grade of C or better in each required course, or a Pass grade if Pass is equivalent to a C. Applicants also need to be in good academic standing at the last college or university attended before enrolling at UC.

Campus, college, and major preparation requirements may be additional. UC recommends using ASSIST to confirm whether California community college courses are UC-transferable and how they apply to requirements.

## Answer guardrails

- Never describe the minimum GPA as a competitive GPA.
- Meeting the minimum transfer GPA or course requirements does not guarantee admission; only TAG offers a guarantee, at participating campuses, if all conditions are met.
- Always distinguish minimum UC eligibility from campus or major selection requirements.
- Do not imply the 7-course pattern alone is enough for admission.
- Do not confirm that a specific course transfers without checking ASSIST or an official campus evaluation.
