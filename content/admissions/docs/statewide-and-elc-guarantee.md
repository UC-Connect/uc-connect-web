# UC Statewide Guarantee and Eligibility in the Local Context (ELC)

UC offers two guarantee paths for California resident first-year applicants who meet minimum UC requirements. Neither guarantees admission to a campus the student chose.

A student who qualifies under either path and is not admitted to any UC campus they applied to may be offered a spot at another UC campus with space available.

## Statewide guarantee

Students qualify if they rank in the top 9 percent of California high school students according to UC's updated Statewide Index.

UC's admissions index instructions estimate statewide index status from two inputs: the UC GPA and the number of A-G semester courses (courses completed in 9th-11th grade plus courses in progress or planned for 12th grade). UC calls the result "just an estimate and not a guarantee." Examples from UC's chart of minimum A-G course counts by GPA:
- GPA 3.60-3.64: 76 A-G semester courses;
- GPA 3.80-3.84: 63;
- GPA 4.00-4.04: 46;
- GPA 4.25 or above: 30.

UC's chart starts at a 3.60 GPA.

## Eligibility in the Local Context (ELC)

Students qualify for ELC if they are California residents, rank in the top 9 percent of their graduating class at a participating California high school, and meet minimum UC requirements.

How ELC is determined:
- UC uses the GPA in UC-approved coursework from 10th and 11th grade, with a minimum GPA of 3.0.
- The GPA is compared to the school's benchmark GPA, which UC sets from transcripts of the top 15 percent of the school's junior class to represent the expected GPA of the top 9 percent.
- Screening is automatic. Students do not submit extra paperwork. After applying, students can check the "View how your application is reviewed" link.

Coursework that must be completed before senior year for ELC: 1 year of history, 2 years of English, 2 years of mathematics, 1 year of science, 1 year of language other than English, and 4 more A-G courses.

ELC status is also one of the factors in comprehensive review.

## Answer guardrails

- Never say ELC or the statewide guarantee guarantees admission to a specific campus or to the student's chosen campuses.
- Do not tell a student they are in the top 9 percent; UC determines this.
- These guarantees apply to California residents only.
- Do not state chart values beyond those listed in the retrieved context.
