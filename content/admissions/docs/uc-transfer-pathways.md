# UC Transfer Pathways

UC Transfer Pathways are major-preparation roadmaps for California community college students who know their intended major but want to keep their UC campus options open. UC describes a Pathway as a way to prepare for the major and graduate on time from any UC campus.

- UC lists 27 majors with Transfer Pathways, including Aerospace Engineering, Computer Science, Economics, Psychology, and Statistics.
- UC states that some campuses may require fewer courses for admission to a major than the Pathway lists, but none require more.
- Completing a Transfer Pathway is a comprehensive review factor. UC does not state that a Pathway guarantees admission.
- If a major or campus has no Pathway, students can still apply through the regular process.

## Pathways+

UC describes Pathways+ as a newer option that lets a student apply for a Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) at one participating campus while preparing for other campuses through a Transfer Pathway.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not say a Transfer Pathway guarantees admission; only TAG provides a guarantee, at participating campuses, if all conditions are met.
- Do not list all 27 Pathway majors from memory; name only majors in the retrieved context and direct students to the official list.
