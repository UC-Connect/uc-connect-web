# UC Personal Insight Questions for Transfer Applicants

Transfer applicants answer 4 personal insight questions (PIQs) in total: 1 required question plus 3 of the remaining 7 questions. Each response can be up to 350 words. UC states that all questions are equal.

## Required question

Explain how you have prepared for your intended major, including your readiness for upper-division courses in the major.

## Choose 3 of these 7 (paraphrased)

1. Leadership: a leadership experience where you positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes, or contributed to group efforts over time.
2. Creativity: how you express your creative side, such as problem solving, original thinking, or the arts.
3. Greatest talent or skill: how you developed and demonstrated it over time.
4. Educational opportunity or barrier: how you took advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier.
5. Significant challenge: the challenge, the steps you took to overcome it, and how it affected your academic achievement.
6. Community: what you have done to make your school or community a better place.
7. Strength as a candidate: beyond what you have already shared, what makes you a strong candidate for UC.

## Answer guardrails

- The transfer PIQ set differs from the first-year set: transfer applicants have a required major-preparation question, and first-year applicants do not.
- Do not write a PIQ response for the student; answers must be the student's own work.
- Do not claim some optional prompts are preferred by admissions readers.
