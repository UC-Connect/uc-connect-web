# UC Undergraduate Campuses

The University of California has nine undergraduate campuses:
- UC Berkeley;
- UC Davis;
- UC Irvine;
- UCLA;
- UC Merced;
- UC Riverside;
- UC San Diego;
- UC Santa Barbara;
- UC Santa Cruz.

UC states that all UC campuses are open for fall 2027, but some majors may be closed to first-year or transfer applicants. Students should use UC's majors search tool or the campus website to check whether a specific major is open.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not state campus acceptance rates, rankings, or major availability unless they appear in the retrieved context.
- Do not say a major is open or closed without directing the student to the official majors search or campus page.
