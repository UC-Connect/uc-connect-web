# UC First-Year Dates and Deadlines

UC application dates change every admission cycle. The official UC first-year dates and deadlines page is the controlling source.

## Fall 2027 first-year timeline

- August 1, 2026: the UC application opens for fall 2027 applicants.
- October 1 - November 30, 2026: fall 2027 application filing (submission) period.
- October 1, 2026: the FAFSA, California Dream Act Application (CADAA), and Cal Grant GPA Verification Form open.
- March 1 - 31, 2027: fall 2027 admission decisions are released. Some campuses may release decisions earlier.
- March 2, 2027: deadline to submit the FAFSA or CADAA and the Cal Grant GPA Verification Form.
- May 1, 2027: Statement of Intent to Register (SIR) deadline for admitted fall 2027 first-year students.
- July 1, 2027: final official transcripts due to the campus for students enrolling in fall 2027.
- July 15, 2027: official AP and IB exam results due.

UC's first-year dates page also lists July 1 - 31, 2026 as the filing period for spring 2027 at select campuses.

## Answer guardrails

- Always state the exact admission term and calendar year.
- Distinguish "application opens" (August 1) from the submission period (October 1 - November 30).
- Do not apply fall 2027 dates to other admission terms or cycles.
- First-year and transfer timelines differ; for transfer dates use the transfer dates source.
