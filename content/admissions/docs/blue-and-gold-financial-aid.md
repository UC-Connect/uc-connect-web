# UC Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan and Financial Aid Forms

## Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan

UC states that most California families earning up to $100,000 receive enough grant and scholarship money to cover UC tuition through the Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan.

- There is no separate Blue and Gold application. Students are automatically considered after submitting the required financial aid forms.
- UC states that the average aid package for these students includes up to $22,000 in grants and scholarships.
- UC states that tuition is set when a student first enrolls and stays the same for up to six years.

## Required forms

Students submit the FAFSA or the California Dream Act Application (CADAA), plus the Cal Grant GPA Verification Form. For the fall 2027 cycle, these forms open October 1, 2026, and the deadline is March 2, 2027.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not promise that a specific family will pay no tuition; Blue and Gold describes most families under the income level, and aid depends on the forms.
- Do not estimate a student's aid amount.
- Do not ask for income records, tax returns, bank details, or Social Security numbers.
