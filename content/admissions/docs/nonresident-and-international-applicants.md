# UC Requirements for Out-of-State and International First-Year Applicants

## Out-of-state (nonresident) first-year applicants

- Nonresidents must meet the same A-G subject requirements as California residents.
- The minimum GPA is 3.4 in A-G courses, with no grade lower than a C. UC describes this as the main difference from requirements for California residents.
- Schools outside California do not have a pre-approved A-G list; UC's A-G course list and the A-G subject descriptions are the guide.
- For the 3.4 calculation, honors weight is given only to AP and IB courses with grades of A, B, or C.
- UC gives priority to California residents, but every campus admits out-of-state students.
- Non-immigrant visa holders are not eligible for California resident status for admission purposes.

## English language proficiency

Applicants who did not complete all of their high school or secondary education in English must demonstrate English proficiency if they have had less than 3 years of instruction in English. A qualifying exam must be completed by December of the final year of high school or secondary school.

Ways to demonstrate proficiency stated by UC:
- AP English Language and Composition or AP English Literature and Composition: score of 3, 4, or 5;
- IB Standard Level English (Language A only): 6 or 7; IB Higher Level English (Language A only): 5, 6, or 7;
- IELTS: 6.5 or higher;
- TOEFL iBT or iBT Home Edition: minimum of 80 for tests taken before January 2026; minimum of 4.5 on the new score scale for tests taken from January 2026;
- Duolingo English Test: 115 or higher;
- ACT English Language Arts: 24 or higher;
- SAT Writing and Language: 31 or higher (the digital SAT Reading and Writing section score is not approved for this requirement);
- a C or better in a UC-transferable English composition course of 3 semester or 4-5 quarter units.

For TOEFL and IELTS, only the highest composite score from a single sitting is used. IELTS One Skill Retake and TOEFL MyBest scores are not accepted.

## Application fee

The application fee is $95 per campus for international and non-immigrant applicants. Fee waivers are limited to U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and applicants eligible for AB 540 benefits.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not state the international applicant process beyond what is listed here; direct students to UC's international applicants section.
- English proficiency scores are minimums, not competitive scores.
- Do not determine a student's residency status; UC makes that determination.
